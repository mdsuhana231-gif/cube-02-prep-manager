# A Note from the Prep Floor

When a unit leaves our prep area, we need to be able to show what we checked and what we saw, not only what the work order asked us to do. If a shipment later arrives with a reported prep defect, the operator may have moved on to many other units. Reconstructing the condition from memory is unreliable and makes it hard to understand or contest what happened.

I want a simple unit-level workflow that keeps the photos, barcode, observations, decision, and any human correction together. The goal is not to replace an operator's judgment. It is to make the review quicker and leave a traceable account of how the decision was reached.

Traceable compliance evidence matters before shipment because once a unit leaves the building, we have less ability to inspect or correct it. A record can help us investigate a later issue and communicate what was observed at prep time. It should be honest about what the system did and did not verify; a mock result or an unclear photo must never be presented as proof that a requirement was met.

*This letter describes the operator perspective the product is intended to serve; it is not a quotation from an interviewed customer.*
